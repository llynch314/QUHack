1. What AttuneMIND is (required)

  • AttuneMIND is a support tool. It is not a therapist, doctor or emergency service, and AI replies can be wrong.
  • If you're in danger, call or text 988 (US) or call 911.
  • Your chats are sent to Anthropic's Claude AI to create replies (voice mode uses Google's Gemini AI). AttuneMIND doesn't save them.
  • We save your Google name and email, and the check-in ratings you choose to save. You can delete everything at any time.

2. Save my expression readings (optional)

If you turn on your camera in Chat, the AI reads your facial expression (for example happy, sad or neutral). If you tick this box, each reading is saved to your account with the time, so you can see how your mood changes in My Progress.

  • Only the expression word is saved, never the camera image. It never identifies you.
  • It's a rough guess, not a diagnosis.
  • You can turn this off or delete all readings in My Progress at any time.
