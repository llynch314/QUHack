Privacy & disclosures

Mind Connect is not therapy or emergency care

Mind Connect is a self-help and support tool. It isn't a licensed therapist, doctor or crisis service, can't diagnose or treat any condition, and can't send help in an emergency. AI replies can be wrong. If you are in danger, call or text 988 (US) or call 911.

What we store

  • If you sign in with Google: your name, email and Google account ID, and the check-in ratings you choose to save.
  • Chats, voice and camera: not stored by Mind Connect. Your conversation is kept only in your browser tab and disappears when you close it.
  • Accessibility settings: saved only in your browser.
  • You can delete your account and all check-ins at any time from My Progress.

AI provider

To create replies, your messages, voice audio and (only if you turn it on) camera snapshots are sent to Google's Gemini API. Depending on the plan the app uses, Google may keep and use this content to improve its products, so please don't include names, addresses, dates of birth or medical record numbers.

Sharing with your care team

Mind Connect never shares anything automatically. You can choose to print or save a report of your check-ins and give it to a therapist, doctor or parent. Once shared, the recipient controls their copy.

HIPAA

Mind Connect is a hackathon prototype and is not HIPAA-compliant. A production version used by healthcare providers would need business associate agreements with every vendor that handles health data (including the AI provider), encryption, access controls and audit logging.
