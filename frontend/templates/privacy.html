Privacy & disclosures

attuneMIND is not therapy or emergency care

attuneMIND is a self-help and support tool. It isn't a licensed therapist, doctor or crisis service, can't diagnose or treat any condition, and can't send help in an emergency. AI replies can be wrong. If you are in danger, call or text 988 (US) or call 911.

What we store

  • If you sign in with Google: your name, email and Google account ID, your account type, the mood colors you pick on your dashboard, the check-in ratings you choose to save, your care team, health documents and alerts.
  • Chats, voice and camera: not stored by attuneMIND. Your conversation is kept only in your browser tab and disappears when you close it.
  • Accessibility settings: saved only in your browser.
  • You can delete your account and all of this data at any time from My account (Settings, in the menu at the top of every page).

AI provider

To create replies, your typed messages and (only if you turn it on) camera snapshots are sent to Anthropic's Claude API. In voice mode, your voice audio and camera snapshots are sent to Google's Gemini API instead. Depending on the plan the app uses, the provider may keep and use this content to improve its products, so please don't include names, addresses, dates of birth or medical record numbers.

Music (Spotify)

The dashboard suggests calm Spotify playlists for the mood color you pick. To find them, attuneMIND sends Spotify only a search phrase (for example "calm acoustic chill"), never your name or account. Spotify's player loads only when you press Play, and Spotify's own privacy policy applies to it.

Phone notifications and text-message alerts

Family members can choose to get alerts as phone notifications through the free ntfy service (ntfy.sh), using a private random topic name they can change at any time, or by text. Their phone number is saved only after they confirm it with a code, and they can remove it at any time. Texts are sent through Twilio and only say who needs support and the kind of alert (for example "Panic attack"), never chat text or notes.

Sharing with your care team

You can add health providers and family members to your Care Team, and remove them at any time. Health providers see only the health documents in your account (including progress reports you choose to add). Family members see only urgent alerts: when you press an alert button, or, if you leave automatic alerts on, when your chat shows signs of a panic attack, meltdown or crisis. Family members never see what you wrote. You can also print or save a report of your check-ins and give it to anyone you choose. Once shared, the recipient controls their copy.

HIPAA

attuneMIND is a hackathon prototype and is not HIPAA-compliant. A production version used by healthcare providers would need business associate agreements with every vendor that handles health data (including the AI provider), encryption, access controls and audit logging.
