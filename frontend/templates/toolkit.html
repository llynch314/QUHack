Quick skills

STOP (DBT)

Stop. Take a step back and breathe. Observe what you feel and what's happening. Proceed mindfully, choosing what helps.

TIPP for intense feelings (DBT)

Temperature: cold water on your face. Intense exercise for a minute. Paced breathing. Paired muscle relaxation: tense, then release.

Opposite action (DBT)

Notice what the emotion urges you to do (hide, snap, avoid), and if it won't help, gently do the opposite: reach out, soften, take one small step.

Check the facts (CBT)

Ask: what's the evidence? What else could explain this? What's most likely? If it did happen, how would I cope?

Self-soothe with your senses

Something soft to touch, a warm drink, a favorite song, a pleasant smell, looking at something calming.

One small step

Feeling stuck? Pick the smallest possible next action - drink water, open a window, send one text - and just do that.

Calming sound

Soft background noise can mask distractions and help you settle. Generated on your device.
