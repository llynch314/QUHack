Expression check (optional)

If you turn this on, camera snapshots are sent to the AI (Claude in text chat, Gemini while talking out loud) so replies can take your expression into account: every 5 seconds in text chat, and about once a second while you're talking out loud. Nothing is saved, and it never identifies you. It's a rough guess, not a diagnosis.
