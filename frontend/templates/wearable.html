Before you connect your watch

  • Your watch sends heart rate, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, steps, active energy, workouts and fall events.
  • They are encrypted in transit (HTTPS tunnel) and encrypted at rest.
  • You see your vitals. Family only get alerts (emergencies, and stress only if you turn it on), never your numbers. Providers only get reports, never live alerts.
  • AttuneMind is not a medical device and never diagnoses. In an emergency it tells you to call 911.
  • You can export or delete your wearable data, and revoke a device, at any time.
